Non-copying boundary
  • Do not copy competitor layout, icon arrangement, brand color, animation signature, wording, or asset.
  • Adopt category principles only: fast capture, optional editing depth, safe recovery, and legible export.
  • All Studio, timeline, inspector, export, and project-history details remain proposed until implemented and tested.
